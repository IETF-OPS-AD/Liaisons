# Description

The IETF has created a new Working Group (WG) on Benchmarking & Performance Measurements (BPM) [1] to develop and maintain metrics, methodologies, and protocols that can be applied to the quality, performance, reliability, and scalability of data delivery services and applications running over public or private networks using IETF technologies. The methodologies cover data, control, and management planes and they apply to hardware, virtualized, and containerized network infrastructure (such as Data Center network fabrics, controllers, routers, and firewalls), including Network Management software systems and endpoints in production and lab environments.
 
The BPM WG is the successor of the now-concluded IETF BMWG and IPPM WGs and maintains the RFCs they published. BPM will thus be the main group fordevelops generic performance measurement protocols, metrics, and benchmarking methodologies, while other IETF WGs remain responsible for technology-specific proceduresand protocols development within the IETF. BPM’s work is limited to IP and upper-layer protocols defined by the IETF. MPLS and Layer 2 are in scope only when generic BPM mechanisms are applied to them. Refer to [1] for an overview of its current milestones and topics.
 
BPM work may be of interest to SA2 (e.g., QoS monitoring, ATSSS performance measurement) and SA5 (e.g., performance measurements, KPIs, and performance-related YANG models). We look forward to fruitful collaboration between your group and the new BPM WG on Benchmarking & Performance matters. The BPM WG Chairs would be delighted to present the work scope of the group during one of your upcoming meetings.
 
The BPM WG encourages the use of the BPM WG mailing list [2] as the most effective and expedient way of exchanging information, answering questions, and progressing any work.
