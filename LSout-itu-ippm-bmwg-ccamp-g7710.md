Due Date: 31 October 2026

The IETF CCAMP/IPPM WGs [1][2] would like to inform the ITU-T SG15 that the WGs are considering adoption of work referencing the G.7710 work. Specifically,
the following documents are candidates for adoption: 

* A YANG Data Model for Collection Measurement (draft-yoon-ippm-collection-measure) [3]
* A YANG Data Model for Collection Interval Capabilities (draft-yoon-ippm-collection-interval-capabilities) [4]
* A YANG Data Model for Performance Monitoring Streaming on Common Transport Equipment (draft-yoon-ccamp-pm-streaming) [4]

Whether a subset or all of these documents are adopted by the IETF WGs will depend on the community feedback.

The CCAMP/IPPM WGs are interested for reviews from ITU-T SG15 about the alignment of these specifications with the G.7710, in particular.

The CCAMP/IPPM WGs encourage the use of IPPM WG mailing list [6] as the most effective and expedient way of exchanging information,
answering questions, and progressing any work.
